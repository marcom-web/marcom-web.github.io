What we collect

We only collect what you type into our forms:

  • Contact forms (top of the page and footer): your name, email address, phone number and, in the footer form, a short message about your project.
  • Project brief (brief.html): your name, email and phone, plus the answers you choose to give about your business, current website, preferred plan, reference websites, launch date and budget.

With each form we also record the date and time, which form you used and the page address it was sent from.

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. It stores two small notes in your browser's session storage so the intro animation and the Christmas offer are only shown once per visit. They stay on your device and are cleared when you close the tab.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry by email or phone.

  • To prepare a plan, timeline and quote for you.

We do not sell your information, and we do not add you to a mailing list.
